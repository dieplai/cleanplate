Remove the visible AI mark.
Keep every other pixel.

cleanplate finds the Gemini / Nano Banana diamond or the Veo mark, measures it on your file and un-blends it exactly; on video it recovers the hidden pixels from neighbouring frames. Everything runs in this tab — nothing is uploaded.
