How it works GitHub

Remove the visible AI mark.
Keep every other pixel.

Drop as many Gemini, Nano Banana and Veo images and videos as you like. Cleanplate measures the mark on each file, un-blends it exactly and rebuilds video from neighbouring frames, on all your CPU cores. Nothing is uploaded.

Runs on your device Many files at once Refuses when unsure Visible marks only
Drop images, videos or whole folders PNG, JPG, WebP · MP4 and MOV (H.264) · paste works too

    1

    Drop everything

    Images and videos, one by one or whole folders. Sub-folders are kept when you save.

    2

    Cleaned in parallel

    On all your CPU cores, two files at a time when your machine has the cores and the memory for it.

    3

    Check, then save

    Compare any result with its original, then download it, download everything as a ZIP, or save straight into a folder.

    Visible marks only: SynthID and C2PA are not touched. Labelling AI-generated content is your responsibility: many places require it by law, for example a visible label on realistic AI images and videos in Vietnam. Use Cleanplate on content you have the right to edit. The method and its benchmark are on GitHub.
